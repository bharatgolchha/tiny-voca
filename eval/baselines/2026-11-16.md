## Reading

- Intelligibility matches Kokoro and the training audio on ordinary sentences
  (about 1% word errors). Naturalness does not: 0.6 to 0.9 below the training
  audio the model imitates, and 0.5 to 1.3 below Kokoro.
- Real audiobook speech (LibriTTS-R) scores 3.31, below the synthetic voices:
  UTMOSv2 favours clean studio-like audio. Listening tests decide phase gates,
  and phase 1 should score candidate training data with UTMOSv2 too.
- The weak spots of the text front end: times, dates, abbreviations, names.
