# Human-speech training corpus, 2026-11-17

All of it is CC BY 4.0, so a model trained on it can be released or sold with attribution. It was built with `scripts/prepare_training_data.sh` on RunPod, and the data lives on network volume zxif79opsx under `/workspace/data/corpus`.

| Stage | Clips | Hours | Speakers |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Every usable clip (LibriTTS-R train-clean-100 and -360, Hi-Fi TTS clean) | 255,565 | 334.8 | 919 |
| After the speaker quality cut (worst 20% by UTMOSv2, 5 clips each; cutoff 3.12) | 190,552 | 262.0 | 736 |
| After alignment checks (train) | 175,270 | 244.7 | 736 |
| After alignment checks (validation) | 1,387 | | |

- **Kept by source (before alignment checks):** Hi-Fi TTS 84.1 h (speakers 92 and 9017; 6097 fell under the cut), LibriTTS-R clean-360 138.0 h, clean-100 39.5 h. Speakers are split 367 female and 367 male, plus the 2 Hi-Fi TTS speakers, which aren't labelled.
- **Left out while listing:** 65,013 clips by the quality cut, 10,008 shorter than 1 s or longer than 20 s, 5,342 from speakers with under 5 minutes of audio, 5,018 with fewer than 2 words, and 189 whose sentence is in a test set.
- **Alignment rejections (train, 7.3%):** 11,269 vowels not voiced, 1,827 overlong phone, 1,061 weak alignment, 444 low confidence, 87 implausible rate. Most are pitch-tracker misses on creaky vowels, the same effect the F2/M2 data showed.
- **Feature preparation:** 189,128 train and 1,424 validation clips, none rejected.
- **Cost:** about $8.40 of RunPod time for steps 1 to 5 (CPU pod $0.48/h for download and features, RTX PRO 4500 $0.72/h for scoring and alignment).

## Before training

The alignment ran before the completeness fix (commit 531ed47), so its `annotations.json` says `"complete": false`. There is no test split to annotate, so set `complete` to true once on the volume. Do this in the first training session, before `tiny_voca.train` reads the manifests.
